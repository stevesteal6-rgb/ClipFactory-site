ClipFactory Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 August 2026

ClipFactory is a personal Windows application. It is not intended to sell personal information or provide data to advertisers.

Information used

The application may process campaign details, source media, transcripts, generated videos, captions, upload status, post URLs, and performance information needed to operate the workflow.

Account access

Connected platform access is granted by you using the platform's authorization flow. Credentials and access tokens are stored locally where possible and are not intentionally shared with third parties.

How information is used

Information is used to discover campaigns, create content, upload authorized posts, avoid duplicate uploads, and track campaign results.

Sharing and retention

Data is sent only to services required for the workflow or explicitly selected by you. You may delete local project data and revoke platform access at any time.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the account owner through the associated service account.
